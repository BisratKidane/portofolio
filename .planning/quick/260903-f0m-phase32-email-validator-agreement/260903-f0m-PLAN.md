<objective>
Close CR-01 from `.planning/phases/32-tokenless-registration/32-REVIEW.md` by making
`assertRegistrationEmail`'s pre-try email guard agree with `models.User`'s `isEmail: true`
model validator BY CONSTRUCTION -- both call the same `validator.isEmail` function -- instead
of approximating it with a hand-rolled regex that a code review empirically proved diverges
on 8 realistic input classes. Also close WR-06 and WR-07: two regression tests in the same
review round that assert weaker/stale invariants than the fixes they exist to guard.

Purpose: CR-01 is the exact silent-drop defect Gap 3 was supposed to close, just narrowed --
a malformed email that passes the guard's over-permissive regex still throws inside the
uniform-response try/catch and gets swallowed into the identical success message, leaving the
registrant waiting forever for a verification email that will never arrive. Two validators for
one field is the root cause; declaring `validator` (already installed transitively via
sequelize) as a direct dependency and calling its `isEmail` from both sides removes the
possibility of drift entirely, rather than re-tuning a second regex that will drift again.
Output: `emailPolicy.js` provably agrees with `models/User.js`'s validator, proven by a
table-driven test against real `models.User` validation (not just the guard's own regex);
`updateUser.test.js` and `verifyEmail.test.js` assert/mirror the actual three-part
functioning-admin predicate instead of a stale two-part one.
</objective>
