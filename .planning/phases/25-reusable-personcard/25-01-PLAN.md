| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-25-01 | Elevation of Privilege | `PersonCard`'s `member.canEdit === true` edit-button gate | accept | This is a client-side UX gate only, not an authorization control — a user could force-render/trigger the affordance via devtools.
