<objective>
Close v4.1 milestone-audit W-1: a self-registrant who verifies their email while still `Pending` currently gets silently bounced through `/pending` to `/login` with no explanation, because `/pending`'s `!user` guard fires on a no-session hit and route `state` does not survive a refresh. This plan adds a dedicated, stateless, session-free `/awaiting-approval` page that a non-Active verifier lands on instead, retargets `VerifyEmail.jsx`'s single `navigate()` call, and retitles `Pending.jsx` (D-07) so it no longer claims to be about approval -- that concept now belongs entirely to the new page.

The plan is written red-first (D-12): the composed test and the final `AwaitingApproval.jsx` are built together in Task 1, but `VerifyEmail.jsx` is deliberately left pointed at `/pending` until Task 2 -- so the first test run is the literal reproduction of W-1 (a Pending verifier lands on the login form), not a simulation of it.

Purpose: honest, refresh-safe post-verify UX for REG-04/REG-06's user-facing half, closing W-1 without touching any resolver, schema, or `AuthContext` behavior (D-04, locked).
Output: `AwaitingApproval.jsx`, `verifyLanding.composed.test.jsx`, the `App.jsx` route, `VerifyEmail.jsx`'s retargeted destination, and `Pending.jsx`'s reworded copy -- all with automated coverage including a recorded red run and recorded mutation-test results.
</objective>
