### Locked copy by surface

These are the exact final strings for the executor to implement verbatim. Wording choices below are `[DEFAULT]` per CONTEXT.md's "Claude's Discretion" grant (exact wording within the stated constraints) — the planner may keep them as-is; any change must still satisfy the constraints cited.

**1. New landing page — `frontend/src/pages/AwaitingApproval.jsx` (D-01, D-02, D-05, D-06)**

| Prop/element | Value |
|---|---|
| `eyebrow` | `Email verified` |
| `title` | `Awaiting admin approval` |
| Body paragraph 1 (`Typography`) | `Your email address is verified. An administrator now reviews your request to join the family.` |
| Body paragraph 2 (`Typography color="text.secondary"`) | `You'll get an email as soon as you're approved — sign in from there.` |
| `footer` | Verbatim copy of `VerifyEmail.jsx:37-43`'s footer: a `Typography variant="body2" color="text.secondary"` wrapping a `Link component={RouterLink} to="/login"` reading **"Return to sign in"**. |

Sequence check (D-05 requires all four beats present): email verified (paragraph 1) → administrator now reviews (paragraph 1) → email sent on approval (paragraph 2) → then sign in (paragraph 2). All four present. Never names the registrant's email address (D-02 — no session, no query param carries it; do not add one). No buttons; no "contact us" line (D-06).

**2. `/pending` retitle — `frontend/src/pages/Pending.jsx` (D-07)**

| Prop/element | Current | New |
|---|---|---|
| `title` | `Waiting for admin approval` | `Waiting to be linked to your family` |
| Body (`Typography`) | `Your account is awaiting an admin to link you to your family member; you'll get access once linked.` | `Your account is approved. An administrator still needs to link you to your family member in the tree — you'll get access as soon as that happens.` |

The word **"approval"** (noun) does not appear in either string. **"approved"** (adjective, stating a completed fact) does appear in the body by design — D-07 explicitly requires the copy to say the account *is* approved, distinguishing this page's state from the new landing page's state. All three `if`/`return`/`<Navigate>` guard lines in `Pending.jsx` stay byte-identical — this is copy-only (Pitfall 2 in RESEARCH.md: do not add a conditional branch that tries to distinguish self-registered vs. invited users; nothing in `user` supports that distinction).

**3. Invited-path success copy — `frontend/src/pages/Register.jsx:310-313` (D-08)**

| | Current | New |
|---|---|---|
| Client paragraph (`Typography variant="body2" color="text.secondary"`, directly below the `Alert severity="success"`) | `We've sent a verification link to your email. After you verify, an administrator will review and approve your account before you can sign in.` | `We've sent a verification link to your email. Verify it and you can sign in right away — no admin review required.` |

**Explicitly UNCHANGED, do not touch:** the `Alert severity="success"` above it renders `{result.message}` — the server's `TOKENLESS_REGISTER_MESSAGE`-equivalent invited-branch string (D-10, see below). Also explicitly UNCHANGED: the self-registration success paragraph at `Register.jsx` ~lines 211-215 (`Once you verify your email, an administrator reviews your request. You'll be able to sign in as soon as they approve it.`) — that promise is correct for the self-registration path (REG-04) and must remain byte-identical. Only the `token`-branch paragraph (lines 310-313) changes. No inviter-vouch sentence is added (considered and dropped per D-08 — the post-submit UI does not know the inviter's name without new plumbing).

**4. Invitation email — `backend/src/services/mailer.js`, `sendInvitationEmail` (D-08)**

| Field | Current | New |
|---|---|---|
| `text` template, trailing sentence (~line 149, after `${noteLine}`) | `After you register, an administrator will review and approve your account before you can sign in.` | `Verify your email and you can sign in right away — no admin review required.` |
| `renderHtml({ note })` (~line 160) | `After registering, an administrator reviews and approves your account before you can sign in.` | `Verify your email and you can sign in right away — no admin review required.` |

Per D-08, all three invited-path locations (Register.jsx client paragraph above, and these two mailer strings) carry the same canonical message — "verify, then sign in, no review". The two mailer strings are byte-identical to each other; the client paragraph adapts the lead-in ("Verify it" referring back to "your email") but ends on the same clause. No new interpolation is introduced — both edits are to static string literals; nothing here requires `escapeHtml()`.

**5. Shared verification email — `backend/src/services/mailer.js`, `sendVerificationEmail` (D-09)**

| Field | Current | New |
|---|---|---|
| `text` template, opening sentence (~line 169) | `Welcome! Please verify your email address to activate your account.` | `Welcome! Please confirm this is your email address.` |
| `renderHtml({ intro })` (~line 172) | `Welcome! Please verify your email address to activate your account.` | `Welcome! Please confirm this is your email address.` |

Path-neutral by design (D-09): this function is called from both the invited and self-registration `register` branches; it must not claim either "activate your account" (untrue for the self-registration path, where verification is only the first of two gates) or an admin-review promise (untrue for the invited path). No signature change, no per-path flag, no branching — one string edit, applied identically in both the `text` and `intro` fields. Nothing else in this function changes (`heading: 'Verify your email'`, `ctaLabel: 'Verify email'`, the 24-hour expiry `note` line all stay).
