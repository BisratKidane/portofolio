<objective>
Close the loop REG-05/REG-06 describe: a self-registration stays invisible to admins until its email is verified, and verifying it both surfaces the account in a new admin-facing query and notifies administrators (D-11, D-12), with the notification recipient list itself tightened to exclude an admin whose own email is unverified (D-13).
