<objective>
Enforce the switch on the server, and prove — three different ways — that the client's outer guard and the server's inner guard cannot diverge.

Purpose: this is the phase's headline risk. The client query and the server check are an outer and an inner guard on the same predicate, and that bug class has produced a blocker in Phases 14, 15, 30, 31, 32 and 33 — most recently *inside the agreement test built to prevent it*. 32-REVIEW.md CR-01 was TDD'd, green, and wrong, because the test asserted only that the two sides agreed. This plan implements all three of RESEARCH Pattern 3's layers: one function with three callers, a call-count proof that the callers share the same function object, and a five-state table asserting **both the absolute value and the agreement**.

Output: a ~12-line guard in `user.resolver.js` and the register-agreement half of `publicRegistrationSwitch.test.js` (plan 34-03 wrote the settings-layer half of the same file).
</objective>
